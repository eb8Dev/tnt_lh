1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Teas N Trees (a cafe) and Cream N Crust (a bakery) are two physical stores in
Guntur, India, run by the same owner. The app lets their customers order food
and drinks from either store for home delivery, and follow the order.

2. MAIN FEATURES
The switch at the top of the home screen changes store. Add items to the cart,
open the cart, choose or add a delivery address, and place the order. Orders
appear under Orders with live status and a map once a rider is assigned.

3. PAYMENTS
Everything sold is physical food and drink, delivered. Payment is cash on
delivery. There is no digital content, so there is no In-App Purchase.

4. LOCATION
Location is used only while the app is open, to set the delivery address and
show the order on the map. It is never tracked in the background.

5. SIGN-IN AND ACCOUNT DELETION
Phone number, Google and Sign in with Apple are offered. Account deletion is
in Profile -> Delete account.
