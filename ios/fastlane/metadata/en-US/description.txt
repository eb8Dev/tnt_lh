BrewCrust brings two Guntur stores together in one app: the Teas N Trees cafe and the Cream N Crust bakery. It is the quickest way to order from either.

TWO STORES, ONE APP
Switch between the Teas N Trees cafe and the Cream N Crust bakery from the top of the home screen. Each has its own menu and its own cart.

FROM THE CAFE
Handcrafted teas, coffee, coolers, snacks and quick bites. Pick a size and add-ons where an item offers them.

FROM THE BAKERY
Cakes priced by weight, with eggless and custom options where the bakery offers them, plus pastries and everyday bakes.

ORDERING THAT STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
- Browse by category, search, or open the full menu
- See what is available right now
- Save the items you like to a wishlist
- Save your delivery addresses, or drop a pin on the map
- Apply a coupon at checkout
- Pay cash on delivery

FOLLOW YOUR ORDER
Watch each order move from confirmed to prepared to out for delivery, and follow the rider on a live map. Past orders are kept so you can reorder or check a bill.

YOUR ACCOUNT
Sign in with your phone number, Google or Apple. You can delete your account from the Profile screen at any time.

Delivery is available within the stores' service area in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh.
